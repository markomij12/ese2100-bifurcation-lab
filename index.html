How to read it

Solid teal branches are attracting equilibria; dashed coral branches are repelling. The gold line is the current μ. Click the vector-field plot to drop a particle. Arrow keys nudge μ; space starts a slow sweep.

Why one dimension

In a 1-D continuous-time system the only generic bifurcations of equilibria are saddle-node, transcritical, and pitchfork. A Hopf bifurcation needs a 2-D phase plane — saved for later. Discrete time (maps) can period-double; this lab stays with flows ẋ = f(x, μ).
